Stratos: Everything We've Reported

Utah's largest proposed data center shows up across many of our repos: the approval, the taxes, the water, the referendum and the 2026 races. Every page is here in one place.

Stratos in brief, from the public record
  • About 40,000 acres of private land plus about 1,279 acres of military and state land in Hansel Valley, Box Elder County.
  • MIDA's board approved the project area unanimously on April 24, 2026.
  • Listed at 7.5 gigawatts (Salt Lake Tribune), and 9 gigawatts at full build-out per the Sierra Club.
  • Master developer: O'Leary Digital Utah Development Co, LLC. The project is branded “Wonder Valley.”
  • MIDA's 0.5% energy tax in the project area begins Oct. 1, 2026 (Ordinance 2026-01).
  • A citizen referendum against it is in court.

MIDA's development agreement, interlocal agreement and development standards for Stratos are posted as drafts and are unsigned, according to MIDA's own document page.
