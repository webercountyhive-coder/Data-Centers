Stratos: Everything We've Reported

Posted Oct. 3, 2026 · Updated Oct. 24, 2026

Utah's largest proposed data center shows up across many of our repos: the approval, the taxes, the water, the referendum and the 2026 races. Every page is here in one place.

Stratos in brief, from the public record
  • About 40,000 acres of private land plus about 1,279 acres of military and state land in Hansel Valley, Box Elder County.
  • MIDA's board approved the project area unanimously on April 24, 2026.
  • Listed at 7.5 gigawatts (Salt Lake Tribune), and 9 gigawatts at full build-out per the Sierra Club.
  • Master developer: O'Leary Digital Utah Development Co, LLC. The project is branded “Wonder Valley.”
  • MIDA's 0.5% energy tax in the project area begins Oct. 1, 2026 (Ordinance 2026-01).
  • A citizen referendum against it is in court.
  • Update, Oct. 24, 2026: the referendum case is before Judge Spencer D. Walsh in 1st District Court. The county and O'Leary Digital asked the judge to dismiss it; the referendum sponsors asked for summary judgment. No ruling had been issued as of Sept. 15, 2026 (Salt Lake Tribune). Nuclear reactors are a permitted use in Stratos's draft development standards; see Utah's Nuclear Projects.
  • Update, Oct. 24, 2026: Stratos filed a water rights change application on Oct. 15, 2026, listing 4,850 employees and a 7.5-gigawatt power facility (Deseret News, Oct. 16). After a June 1 letter from MIDA's chair asking for a 75% reduction, more than 20,000 acres were removed from the footprint. Box Elder's 180-day data center moratorium, Ordinance 654, cannot reach the project. Two defamation suits have been filed against Kevin O'Leary and Fox News over claims about Stratos critics: one in July by Alliance for a Better Utah and others, and one on Oct. 14 by Taylor Knuth of Ogden (KSL, Oct. 15). Data center water reporting is covered on H.B. 76; the acreage and moratorium on Eight Project Areas.
  • Read the article: The Shield and Fragment Strategy (Substack, Aug. 7, 2026).

MIDA's development agreement, interlocal agreement and development standards for Stratos are posted as drafts and are unsigned, according to MIDA's own document page.
